Know Wild Brazil...

We are a young and dynamic NGO, located in Bahia - Brazil, dedicated to protecting Brazil's unique biodiversity through direct conservation, community education, and the active fight against animal mistreatment and illegal wildlife trafficking. We believe that safeguarding our wildlife starts with raising awareness, protecting vulnerable species, and stopping exploitation.

As an independent organisation, we rely heavily on community support. Your donation is vital to keeping our rescue operations and educational programmes active, protecting threatened habitats, and securing a future for our wildlife.

We also believe in the power of collective action, working closely alongside other non-governmental organisations like WWF, RENCTAS, Projeto Tamar, and Instituto Fauna Brasil to maximise our impact on the ground.
